## WEEK 5 — STATUS: [✓] VERIFIED COMPLETE
**Task:** tune the selected model, robustness, documented experiment table, error analysis.

**Evidence produced**
- 14-configuration study with 5-fold CV each: learning rate, batch size, augmentation strength, dropout, weight decay, label smoothing, class weighting, optimiser, schedule, a combination run, and resolution-degradation augmentation.
- Focal loss was deliberately not run: the classes are balanced (documented).
- Run-to-run noise quantified (~0.01).
- Error analysis found that the model's output was sensitive to image resolution (an acquisition shortcut): 46% of correct benign images flipped to "malignant" after down-sampling.
- Experiment E13 (resolution-degradation augmentation) reduced this to 2% at similar CV macro-F1, and was selected under a rule amended before the remaining results were inspected.
- Leakage replication: P12-style training gives 1.000 accuracy vs 0.788 when copies are removed (0.981 for the size control).
